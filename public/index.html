Water-logging, blockades and jams reported by commuters, weighted by how many people confirm them.

Alert Ingestor: paste a street post

A messy Bangla, Banglish or English post becomes a structured alert that starts at low trust (0.30, "unverified"). It only slows routes a little until other people confirm it; one post never blocks a road on its own.
